    Building the scene

    Click a piece to select it, then drag to move it. With the canvas focused, arrow keys move the selection, Shift with an arrow moves it further, Delete removes it and Escape deselects. Control plus the mouse wheel zooms; the wheel, or Space and drag, pans. The Layers list and the inspector do everything the mouse does.
